## Notes

Upcoming edition. `design-cdl-2026.svg` is the line-art
illustration (Capitole, Ariane rocket, Tux and the GNU on the
Occitan cross) shared by the poster, t-shirt and social visuals.

Available so far: poster, t-shirt front, announcement and
programme visuals, social-media banners (Twitter/X, GitHub, Pretix)
and the CFP campaign visuals (`social/cfp-*`: reminders, last day,
closing, answers). Signage will be added as it is produced.

`poster/affiche-cdl-2026-v2.*` reuses the v1 layout with the
t-shirt front artwork and adds a sponsor band (sponsors as listed on
capitoledulibre.org on 2026-10-29).

`social/speaker-*` is a ready-to-use kit for speakers ("J'interviens
au Capitole du Libre"): X/Mastodon/Bluesky header (1500×500),
LinkedIn banner (1584×396), landscape post / link preview
(1200×630), square post (1080×1080), story (1080×1920) and e-mail
signature (1200×300 PNG, to display at 600×150). PDFs have text
converted to paths; SVGs keep live text and need the Ubuntu font.
