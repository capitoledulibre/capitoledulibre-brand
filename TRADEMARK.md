# Logo and name usage

The contents of this repository are released under
[CC BY 4.0](LICENSE), which permits broad reuse. The
"Capitole du Libre" name and logo are part of the event's identity,
and reuse should not give the false impression that an unrelated
project, product or event is organized, endorsed or sponsored by
Capitole du Libre.

This document describes how to use the name and logo. It complements
the license; it does not override it.

## Uses that do not require prior permission

- Editorial coverage: news articles, blog posts, talks, podcasts and
  social-media posts mentioning or covering the event.
- Linking to capitoledulibre.org with the logo as the link.
- Slides and posters announcing your participation in (or a talk you
  are giving at) a specific edition of Capitole du Libre.
- Educational and academic material referring to the event.

In all of the above, please:

- Use the logo as provided, without altering its colors, proportions
  or typography. Resizing proportionally is fine.
- Keep enough surrounding space so the logo remains legible.
- Credit the source as suggested in the [README](README.md#license).

## Uses that require prior written permission

- Use that implies endorsement, sponsorship or an official
  partnership when none exists.
- Merchandise (t-shirts, stickers, mugs, ...) sold to the public.
- Use as part of the name, logo or branding of another project,
  product, company or event.
- Modified versions of the logo (recolored, stylized, combined with
  other marks, used as a base for derivative logos).
- Domain names, social-media handles or app names containing
  "Capitole du Libre" or a confusingly similar variation.

## Requesting permission

For any use listed above, or if you are unsure, please contact the
organizing team at **contact@capitoledulibre.org** with a short
description of the intended use. We try to answer quickly and we are
generally happy to say yes when the use is in line with the spirit
of the event.

## Reporting misuse

If you notice a use of the name or logo that seems misleading or
abusive, please let us know at the same address.
